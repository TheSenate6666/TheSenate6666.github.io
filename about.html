About Me

Hi! I'm Max, a game design student and developer passionate about creating engaging first-person experiences. I specialize in Unreal Engine 5 development, with a focus on systems design, player mechanics, and creating seamless gameplay through innovative design approaches.

I'm currently working on my Bachelor's thesis in game design, focusing on "invisible tutorials" — teaching game mechanics through intelligent level design rather than explicit instruction. This work directly informs my approach to creating intuitive, player-friendly experiences.

My technical expertise includes:

  • Unreal Engine 5 (Blueprint & C++)
  • Gameplay Ability System (GAS)
  • Level Design & World Building
  • Player Feedback Systems
  • Team-based Game Development
  • Game Design Documentation

When I'm not working on games, you can find me enjoying live music and exploring new festivals. I believe that the same principles that make great interactive experiences also apply to live entertainment — creating moments that resonate with audiences.

I'm always excited to collaborate with fellow developers, designers, and creators. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss game design, technology, or potential projects!
